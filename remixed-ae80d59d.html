Our Mission

Empowering South Asian women entrepreneurs to thrive.

South Asian women carry generations of resilience into every venture they build — and yet, too often, we navigate that journey without a network that truly understands us. SAWE was born to change that.

From first-generation founders launching their first idea, to seasoned entrepreneurs scaling across continents, we share a particular set of challenges: the weight of family expectation, the cultural conversations that don't fit neatly into mainstream business advice, the experience of being the only one in the room. SAWE is the room where you are not the only one.

Founded by Lopa Banerjee, our community exists to connect, mentor, and elevate South Asian women entrepreneurs at every stage. We are a space where ambition is expected, vulnerability is welcomed, and your full story — heritage and all — is an asset, not something to translate.
